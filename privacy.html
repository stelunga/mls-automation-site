Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026.

MLS Automation requests only send-only Gmail permission to deliver user-configured reports. It does not read inbox messages, contacts, or Google profiles, and does not request your Google password.

The app uses authorization tokens to authenticate and refresh sending access. It sends the configured sender and recipient addresses, subject, message, and report attachments to Google for delivery. It uses the sending response to record delivery status. Recipients receive the emailed report; Google and recipient mail services handle their copies.

Authorization data is stored locally in the operating system’s secure credential storage. Settings, report files, and delivery history remain locally until the user removes them. There is no central service collecting report files or tokens.

You can stop using the app, remove its local authorization and files, and revoke access through your Google Account connections. Local deletion does not revoke Google access or erase delivered email. For support, use the support contact displayed on the Google consent screen.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models. Use and transfer of Google API information adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

This static website has no forms or tracking scripts. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
